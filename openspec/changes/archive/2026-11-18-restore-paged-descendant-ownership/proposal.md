## Why

Paged resume can display a persisted child but omit its detail route. Nested delegation stores the lifecycle in the root Timeline, and an early failure can end before a child result exists. Both facts belong to the existing recovery authority.

## What Changes

Resolve children selected by the adopted history page at its captured cut, including canonical ancestors. Apply recorded terminal state across the tree and preserve a confirmed launch failure without inventing child history or a result. Page selection remains available when optional session-load metadata exceeds its 1 MiB budget.
