## Why

A shrink to a smaller model can leave its destination window exhausted after one committed compaction. The completed turn releases foreground ownership. A durable background receipt can then start an independent turn, reset turn-local compaction suppression, and hold the model control boundary in another summary request. Goal has its own autonomous foreground entry point and can also rearm idle scheduling without new recovery evidence.

## What Changes

- Record the failed effective window in the canonical typed Turn terminal and project it through Timeline replay and the published cut.
- Make autonomous model admission use this evidence and the active destination's effective request pressure and known output reserve. Retain notification receipts when admission is blocked.
- Keep explicit recovery actions available. Verify real actor recovery, Goal admission and durable resume.
