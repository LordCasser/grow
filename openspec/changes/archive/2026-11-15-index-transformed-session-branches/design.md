## Adversarial cases

- A process may stop between the canonical rewind event and its presentation marker, after a derived transaction but before manifest rename, or after rename but before ACK. A UI marker alone cannot confer branch authority.
- Rewind can reuse prompt coordinates and preserve earlier responses that compaction shadowed. Branch-local lookup cannot return a discarded predecessor at a reused prompt coordinate. Physical identity deduplication still includes discarded facts.
- A projected image may belong to a compacted leaf, Tool result, call or carrier. The saved Surface must equal the full canonical projection, including all replacements with original item coordinates. Full audit and transfer still resolve original evidence.
- A corruption on a needed proof must fail recovery or a page read. A closed unrelated branch cannot contribute to resume, page or exit read volume.
