## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Working publications cover rewind and image projection

A working publication SHALL bind the selected transformed Surface, item identities and revision to the canonical transformation and published journal frontier. It SHALL preserve exact execution identity, pending ownership, and branch-selected prompt coordinates while leaving closed facts available by original identity. Image-projection source, description proof and redaction relationships SHALL remain canonically verifiable.

#### Scenario: A rewind selected earlier history
- **WHEN** an acknowledged rewind is covered by the working publication
- **THEN** cold recovery restores the selected Surface and branch-local prompt admissions. Discarded facts retain their physical identities and cannot occupy the next branch-local prompt coordinate. Subsequent explicit rewind and exact audit can read original evidence.

#### Scenario: An image projection is covered by publication
- **WHEN** the committed projection is covered by the working publication
- **THEN** cold recovery restores the validated Surface including causal item identities and all image, carrier, call and compaction redactions. Original image and Sideband evidence remain available for exact audit. Closed unrelated facts do not enter working recovery.

#### Scenario: A process stops around branch publication
- **WHEN** the writer stops before or after manifest publication or before a rewind display marker finishes
- **THEN** recovery resolves the committed canonical result once and preserves original identities. An unproven or partial publication cannot open admission. A subsequent reopen uses the repaired published cut without rescanning the branch prefix.

#### Scenario: Unrelated closed history grows
- **WHEN** sessions with a published transformed branch have the same working state and unpublished suffix and differ only by unrelated closed history
- **THEN** cold recovery, the first ordinary operation and confirmed exit do not read or replay work proportional to that history.
