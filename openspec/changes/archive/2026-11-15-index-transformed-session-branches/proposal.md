## Why

Grow 2.6.13 uses a working-state publication for ordinary cold resume. A committed rewind or image projection bypasses that publication and reopens the whole Timeline. The work grows with closed history even when the current Surface and unapplied suffix are fixed.

## What Changes

- Publish the validated result of a rewind or image projection in the working-state generation.
- Keep the canonical event history and the selected display order independently addressable by their original coordinates.
- Advance subsequent writes incrementally. Validate and repair an interrupted branch publication from the canonical Timeline.
- Test actual process loss at both publication boundaries and compare three terminal fixture sizes through at least 515 MB for both branches.
