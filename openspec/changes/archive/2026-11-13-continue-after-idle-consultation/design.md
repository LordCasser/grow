## Admission

At successful live synchronization, inspect the answering actor's idle and execution gates while serializing receipt admission. Record idle continuation eligibility on the same durable receipt. Wake the existing idle arbiter after receipt confirmation. A queued user input or foreground owner takes precedence. Goal and suspension gates retain their authority.

Use the existing internal notification Turn and typed AgentMessage consumption. Duplicate delivery and later UI replay cannot create another receipt or Turn. Context received during active work follows the existing sampling and final-response boundaries. A cold repair of a missing receipt supplies passive context because it did not witness live idle admission.
