# Design

Keep the existing event channel and synchronous attempt-discard guard, but put a per-session credit budget on the only unbounded class: L2 content fragments. Charge text/reasoning, tool-call delta, response-start strings, and reasoning signature. One permit represents 16 KiB, with at least one permit per event and 4096 permits total. This bounds queued fragments by count and by their charged payload size to roughly 64 MiB. The provider raw-response evidence cap already prevents an individual L2 fragment from exceeding the full budget.

The `SamplerActor` carries an optional budget into `run_request_task` and `drive_l2`; real Shell sessions supply it, while standalone sampler consumers may supply their own policy. `drive_l2` acquires credits with `tokio::select!` against the attempt cancellation token before the existing unbounded send. After a successful send it transfers ownership of those credits to the channel. If the receiver is closed, the local permit drops. The Shell drainer releases the exact event cost upon receive, before awaiting `handle_sampling_event`, leaving at most one additional fragment in active delivery. Thus slow client/persistence delivery backpressures the provider stream rather than creating another queue.

Small control and terminal events are not per-fragment and retain the current synchronous send paths, especially `AttemptPreview::drop`. Do not coalesce across channel, attempt, or tool boundaries: chunk indexes and eventId order remain unchanged. Test a stalled drainer with finite L2 fragments, cancellation during credit wait, FIFO terminal/discard order, and the existing preview projection behavior.
