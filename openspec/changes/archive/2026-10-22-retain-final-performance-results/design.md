# Design

Use each archived `verification.md` (or the profiling change's `analysis.md`) as the durable final result. Retain OpenSpec proposal, design, tasks, specs, and final review documents. Remove generated per-run data, logs, casts, diagnostic snapshots, benchmark scripts, and intermediate reports from the selected performance archives. Replace links to removed artifacts with plain descriptions and explicitly note the retention boundary. Check local links and OpenSpec validation after cleanup.
