## Scope limits

Receipt retries use a bounded 5-second observation window (up to three attempts where the protocol applies); finalization has one absolute 30-second cycle deadline across queries/retries, reset only by a newly admitted cycle. Tools virtual-clock coverage and Shell caller-deadline/lease coverage passed. Storage recovery is gated: the prior writer must drain its active filesystem operation; a legal writer syncs the recoverable prefix before reopening admission, and a failed barrier keeps admission closed. JSONL lease/sync and `storage_admission` tests cover those boundaries separately. No single fixture combines the full retry deadline with child/parent crash, prolonged storage failure and recovered sync; the canonical commit-boundary cases have passed, but the combined deadline+crash+storage-failure sequence is not claimed. The pure-memory output budget is directly covered by the Tools 8 MiB retention test; process exit before durable commit can still lose volatile-only bytes.
